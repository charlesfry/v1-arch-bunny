# 4.14 — a quotable kitty PSS: the steady-state method (pre-registered)

Problem (4.8b, DA round 2): solo-kitty PSS read 72.8, 137.9, 171.5, 181.2,
262.6 MB across measurements the same day — "no absolute is quotable until
a steady-state method exists." Hypothesis: PSS of a *young* kitty window is
dominated by how much of the shared-library/GPU mappings have faulted in
and how many other processes share them; it grows with window age and with
session history, so any single-instant read is meaningless without (age,
history, boot state) stated.

## Pre-registered protocol (committed before the run)

2 fresh boots (mainline, nvidia present, 45 s settle). Per boot:
- W1 = first kitty of the boot, solo window. PSS sampled from
  `smaps_rollup` on the SAME pid at ages 5 / 30 / 60 / 120 / 300 s.
- W1 killed; W2 spawned (same boot, spawn history of exactly one prior
  kitty). PSS at ages 5 / 60 s.

Decision rule:
- If W1's PSS at 300 s is within 10% of its 120 s reading on both boots,
  the **method rule adopted** is: "kitty PSS is quoted at window age
  >=120 s, first window of a fresh boot, solo, nvidia state stated" — and
  that number becomes the BUDGET.md row.
- If it is still growing >10% between 120 and 300 s, no absolute is
  adopted; the growth curve is deposited and the row stays unquotable.
- W2-vs-W1 at matched age measures the history effect; informational.
- Cross-boot agreement at matched age within 15% required for any adopted
  number; otherwise unquotable.

- **The pre-registered rule fires**: W1 at 300 s is within 3.4% of its

- **Discovery, informational: the boot's FIRST kitty is the anomaly.** A
  second kitty — equally solo — reads **~250-254 MB, 2.4× W1 at matched
  age**, reproduced across boots within 0.5%. "First window of a fresh
  boot" is the single largest determinant of kitty's PSS on this box,
  larger than the nvidia-stack effect (4.8b). Mechanism NOT established
  (candidates worth testing later: warm page cache faulting more of the
  mapped files resident; on-disk shader/glyph caches created by the first
  instance; nvidia GSP buffer reuse) — recorded as an open question, no
  guess enters the ledger.
- This retroactively explains 4.8b's "unquotable" spread: every historical
  sample mixed window ordinal, age, and ICD state.

## Adopted method rule (DA round: survives with caveats; fixes applied)

"kitty PSS" is quotable ONLY as: **first kitty window of a fresh boot,
spawned early in the boot (~60 s uptime — the recipe actually executed),
solo, sampled inside the age WINDOW 120-300 s, ICD state stated** — on
that method, kitty (nvidia maps present) reads **102.3-106.0 MB PSS**
(the band, both bounds deposited; the pre-registration never pinned which
age point becomes the quote — DA #10). This is a **comparability
convention, not "kitty's RAM cost"** — DA's sharpest point: the user's
real kitty is never the boot's first window, and if the ~250 MB
second-window regime is what a mid-session kitty pays, quoting 102-106
alone is the flattering half. Both regimes travel together wherever this
number goes (BUDGET.md carries both).

Also corrected per DA: the pre-registered hypothesis ("PSS grows with
window age") was **refuted in sign** for W1 — it decays monotonically and
is still falling at 300 s, so "slowly decaying" is what was measured, not
a proven steady state (hence an age window, not a floor). The 4.8b spread
is "**consistent with**" mixed ordinal/age/ICD — not "explained": 4.8b's
deposited 138 MB point (solo, ~130 s uptime, high window ordinal) fits
neither story cleanly. The design confounds window ordinal with
uptime-at-spawn (W2 was necessarily spawned at ~6.5 min uptime); n=2
boots establishes repeatability of one recipe, not robustness.

## 4.14b — discriminating probe (pre-registered; instrument committed as `instruments/4.14b-pss-probe.sh`)

Boot C: the boot's FIRST kitty spawned at ~420 s uptime. If it reads
within 20% of ~250 MB, the driver variable is **uptime-at-spawn** and the
rule text above is wrong as written; within 20% of ~105-115 MB, the
**ordinal** story holds. Boot D: W1-early and W2 again, capturing the
`Pss_Anon/Pss_File/Pss_Shmem` split — if W2's +150 MB is dominantly
`Pss_File`, it is warm-page-cache accounting (evictable; arguably not a
2b cost at all); anon growth would be a real cost. Split percentages
quoted informationally, no threshold pre-set.

## 4.14b results (raw appended to the same jsonl; pending DA round 2)

- **Boot C** (`de5929…`-predecessor, first kitty spawned at 420 s uptime):
  **258.7 MB total = 54.2 anon + 204.2 file + 0.3 shmem.** Within 20% of
  the ~250 MB regime → **the pre-registered rule fires for
  uptime-at-spawn**: page-cache warmth at spawn time, not window ordinal,
  is the driver. 4.14's rule text was wrong as written (DA r11 #1
  vindicated).
- **Boot D** (spawned at ~15 s uptime — a runner deviation from the ~60 s
  recipe, disclosed; the colder cache shows exactly as predicted):
  W1 94.4 → 78.4 MB (anon 50.77 → 50.78, file 43.3 → 27.3);
  W2 175.9 → 167.6 MB (anon 50.79 → 50.79, file 124.8 → 116.6).
- **Corrected per DA round 2 (#1, #2): split data exists for THREE windows
  on TWO boots** (C-W1late, D-W1, D-W2), n=1 each — run 2's four windows
  are totals-only. On those three: `Pss_Anon` reads **50.8 MB (cold
  regime, flat to 20 kB across ages) to 54.2 MB (warm regime, and it grew
  52.1→54.2 with age there — a ~3.4 MB warm-regime anon excess, open,
  unexplained)**. The rest of every dramatic number in this saga is
  `Pss_File`. The file-PSS-vs-spawn-uptime relation is monotone across the
  four available doses (15 s/43 MB, ~140 s/~125 MB, ~390 s/~200 MB
  inferred totals-only, 420 s/204 MB) — **strongly indicated as page-cache
  warmth, not established**: the causal counterfactual (`drop_caches` then
  spawn late) was not run, what warmed 204 MB of kitty-mapped files during
  420 idle seconds is undeposited (a timer suspect — journal excerpt
  owed), and W1's *decay* on an idle, pressure-free box is described, not
  explained (reclaim needs pressure; something else is going on).
- **BUDGET consequence, DA-corrected: ~51 MB is the fresh-solo-idle ANON
  FLOOR, not "kitty's real cost."** Untested beyond it: additivity across
  concurrent windows, and growth under real work (scrollback and glyph
  atlases are anon/shmem). File pages are NOT "not a 2b cost" — 2b is
  total RAM in use and resident file pages are in use; the honest framing
  is anon ≈ incompressible floor, file ≈ reclaimable under pressure *at a
  refault-latency price on the 2a path*. The convention clause must PIN
  spawn uptime ("60±10 s"), not bound it (boot D's own 15 s-vs-60 s runs
  differ 26% *inside* a "≤60 s" band), and **the 102-106 MB band has no
  split and is unquotable under the corrected convention until W1-at-60±10 s
  is re-measured with the split instrument.** The boot-D deviation was an
  instrument defect, not a runner slip: the committed script's D arm never
  implemented its own "~60 s" comment (no wait, no assert — a rule-5
  violation one round after rule 5 was written).

## FINAL STATE after DA round 3 (survives with caveats; all corrections applied)

- **The 102.3-106.0 MB comparability band is RETIRED: failed replication**
  — under the nominally pinned recipe, 4.14c's three boots read
  85.6-89.9 MB in the 120-300 s window, 16% below the band. Totals are
  dose- and epoch-sensitive (and deterministic given both: E×3 agree to
  0.3%); **no kitty total is quotable, ever, without its full recipe.**
- **Anon**: spawn-time value **50.74-50.79 MB, n=6 deposited readings
  across two image epochs, within 44 kB** (E×3, D-W1, D-W2, F). It drifts
  to 53.7-54.2 MB by age 300 s in every cold-regime boot (by 60 s in the
  warm boot C); **no sample exists past 300 s, so the long-lived anon
  figure is right-censored: ≥54 MB.** BUDGET leads with that; 50.7-50.8
  is the transient spawn value.
- **Mechanism**: page-cache warmth **causally demonstrated (n=1,
  pre-registered, 3.4× effect)** — the drop_caches boot abolished the
  ~145-155 MB warm excess at matched uptime (204 → 59.7 MB file; note
  59.7 is 21% above cold-boot E's 49.4 — `drop_caches` cannot evict
  pages mapped by running processes, so residual warmth survives; and
  the warm comparator is from the earlier image epoch). "Established"
  needs F×2 more with a same-epoch warm control. **The warmth source is
  undetermined** — the deposited idle-window journal shows no candidate
  that reads ~155 MB of kitty-mappable files; open.
- **Process findings, on the record**: (a) the 60±10 s pin was satisfied
  in letter and defeated in spirit — the poll-until-50 start gun put all
  three boots at 50.8±0.1 s, so within-pin sensitivity was never
  measured; a pin must fix its variable at a stated point or sample
  across the band. (b) 4.15 install work ran on the measurement box
  between 4.14b and 4.14c — a rule-8 asymmetry recorded here after DA
  excavated it; deposits now carry image identity per boot. (c) Rule 1
  was violated three times today (77 s, 25 s, 18 s margins); amended in
  METHODOLOGY rather than argued past a fourth time.
