## 2026-08-25 (overnight): the fuzzel +1 ms question CLOSED — attributed compositor-side by elimination

The client-side hypothesis is **falsified directly**: a live fuzzel spawned
via niri IPC maps **zero** nvidia/EGL/GL/mesa libraries (`/proc/<pid>/maps`
swept for all of them — nothing), and `ldd /usr/bin/fuzzel` (1.14.1-2)
confirms it links only cairo+pixman — fuzzel is a pure software/shm Wayland
client with **no GL surface to pay a glvnd tax on**. That leaves the two
ends of the spawn→layer-mapped path: the client (exonerated) and the
compositor — which is exactly the component 4.8/4.12 showed changing
(73 nvidia mappings, ~8 MB, post-driver). Consistency sample tonight:
warm median **21.9 ms**, n=19 (21.0–24.9), load 0.0, fresh boot — in line
with the post-nvidia record (oddity recorded, not explained: the labeled
cold run 0 measured 14.2 ms, *faster* than warm; single run, no claim).
**Disposition: attributed compositor-side by elimination; size ~1 ms;
accepted cost** — the author already declined ICD pinning for kitty's
7–10 ms, and the only sharper isolation (restarting niri itself with a
mesa-pinned EGL vendor and re-benching) costs a session teardown for a
question no decision hinges on. Off the open list.
