# 4.16 — kitty anon drift, long run (closing 4.14's right-censoring)

**Question.** 4.14 established the kitty anon floor (~50.8 MB at spawn,
n=5 boots, 40 kB agreement) and saw drift to ~54 MB "within minutes", but
**no sample existed past age 300 s** — the long-lived anon figure was
right-censored at ≥54 MB. BUDGET.md's resident-RAM row needs the
steady-state number, not a lower bound.

**Method.** `instruments`-style sampler (`t-4.16-drift.sh` on the box,
shellcheck-clean, helpers copied verbatim from `4.14c-pss-probe.sh`): one
kitty window spawned via niri IPC on a fresh Arch boot (spawn at uptime
92 s), left idle (shell prompt, no input), `smaps_rollup` Pss split
sampled at ages 5/30/60/120/300 s then every 300 s to age 10,800 s (3 h).
Run started 2026-08-24 16:50, boot id `78f402de`. Disclosure: the box was
NOT otherwise idle for the whole window — Phase-4 draft editor testing ran
in other windows during roughly the first 25 minutes (separate processes;
anon is per-process, file-PSS sharing effects disclosed below if visible).

**Pre-registration (written at age ~900 s, before the bulk of the data;
amended-rule-1 compliant).**

- **"Plateau"** (the 4.14-friendly outcome): every sample from age 600 s
  to 10,800 s stays within **±1 MB** of the age-600 s anon value
  (54.332 MB). BUDGET quotes the plateau value as the idle-window anon
  figure.
- **"Slow drift"** (real 2b cost): monotonic-ish growth totaling **>1 MB**
  over the window. Quote MB/h and flag for a longer (overnight) rerun
  before BUDGET adoption.
- **"Step"**: any single-interval jump >2 MB — investigate against the
  journal for the trigger before concluding anything.
- File-PSS is recorded but NOT a decision input (4.14 proved it is
  page-cache accounting, causally, via drop_caches).

**Spawn-floor replication (already in evidence):** anon at age 5 s =
50.788 MB — within 40 kB of 4.14's five-boot floor. The 4.14 method
replicates on a sixth boot.

## Results (run completed 19:50:53; raw: `raw/4.16.drift.log`, 40 samples)

Anon took exactly **three values in three hours**:

| anon (kB) | samples | window ages |
|---|---|---|
| 50,788 | 4 | 5-120 s (the 4.14 spawn floor, replicated to 0 kB) |
| 54,192 | 1 | 300 s (the settling transition) |
| **54,332** | **35** | **600 s → 10,800 s, bit-identical throughout** |

**Verdict: PLATEAU, by the pre-registered margin and then some** — the
requirement was ±1 MB from the age-600 s value; the observed excursion was
**zero bytes across 2 h 50 m**. The 4.14 "drift" is a one-time settling
step (+3.5 MB, complete by age 600 s), not ongoing growth. File-PSS
meanwhile wandered 44-84 MB with system activity, re-confirming 4.14's
page-cache-accounting result without being a decision input.

**BUDGET consequence (applied):** the idle-window figure is
**54.33 MB anon steady-state** per kitty window; the ">=54 MB
right-censored" hedge is retired.

## DA round (self-run, per the standing gate)

1. *"The box wasn't idle — your p4-draft testing and the alert test ran
   during the window."* True and disclosed in-method: activity spanned
   ~16:50-17:10 and ~18:50 (spawning kitties, LazyVim sync, du over $HOME,
   notifications). **Anon never moved through any of it** — the confound
   pushes the wrong way for the plateau to be an artifact of quiet; a
   busy system failing to budge anon *strengthens* the claim. File-PSS
   visibly responded to the same activity, proving the meter was live.
2. *"One boot, one window."* Correct — this closes the censoring, not
   boot-to-boot variance. The spawn floor has n=7 across two image epochs;
   the plateau value has n=1 boot. If a future boot lands a different
   plateau, that is a new fact, not a contradiction of this one.
3. *"Three hours is not a workday."* Also correct: growth slower than
   ~0.3 MB/h would be invisible here. The claim is scoped to "no drift at
   the hours scale"; an overnight run is the natural escalation if BUDGET
   ever needs the days scale.
4. *"The window was idle-shell; a working terminal may behave differently."*
   Scoped deliberately — idle cost is what the resident-RAM bucket prices.
   Working-set behavior is a different question (4.14's scrollback/atlas
   notes cover the mechanism).

**Survives, narrowed to: one boot, one idle window, hours scale.** Tally
row 14 in `benchmarks/DA-TALLY.md`; methodology rules followed
(pre-registered margins, wall-clock deposit, disclosed confounds).
